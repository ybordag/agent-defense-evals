## Reference policy

The final presentation and research report should prefer primary papers and official documentation. Claims should distinguish:

- established mathematical results;
- conditional formal guarantees;
- empirical results reported by prior work;
- new conjectures proposed by this project;
- evidence produced by the project’s own experiments.

New sources should be added here only after confirming that they directly support a claim made elsewhere in the documentation.
